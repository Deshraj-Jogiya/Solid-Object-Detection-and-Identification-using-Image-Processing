# Findings

Measured results from this project, re-run with `python verify_accuracy.py`. The images are generated by `src/dataset.py` with seed
42 (10,000 shapes across 7 classes); the results are on the 2,000 held-out images.

## How accurate is the CNN classifier?

**How it was tested.** The trained PyTorch CNN (`models/shape_detector.pth`) classified the 2,000 held-out images; CI fails if
accuracy drops below the target.

| Accuracy | Target | Mean IoU |
|---|---|---|
| 99.20% | 98.97% | 0.9031 |

| Class | Precision | Recall | F1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Circle | 1.0000 | 0.9930 | 0.9965 |
| Square | 0.9873 | 1.0000 | 0.9936 |
| Triangle | 1.0000 | 1.0000 | 1.0000 |
| Rectangle | 1.0000 | 0.9860 | 0.9930 |
| Pentagon | 0.9794 | 0.9828 | 0.9811 |
| Hexagon | 0.9779 | 0.9815 | 0.9797 |
| Star | 1.0000 | 1.0000 | 1.0000 |

**Conclusion.** The CNN clears the target; pentagons and hexagons, the two shapes closest in outline, have the lowest F1.

**Source.** Output of `verify_accuracy.py` ("Model performance report").

**Skills shown.** PyTorch (CNN classification and box regression), OpenCV, per-class precision/recall/F1.

## Which preprocessing locates the shape better: a thresholded mask or Canny edges?

**How it was tested.** For every held-out image, the box around the largest contour from each method was compared with the true box.

| Method | Mean box IoU | Images with IoU of 0.9 or more |
|---|---|---|
| Thresholded mask | 0.8185 | 15.6% |
| Gaussian blur + Canny edges | 0.9318 | 89.8% |

**Decision.** Canny edges locate each shape before the CNN classifies its crop. The same run shows the CNN reaches 65.65% on
Canny-located crops, against 99.20% on the full images, so the crop step is the place to improve next.

**Source.** Output of `verify_accuracy.py` ("Localization" and "CNN accuracy on Canny-located crops").

**Skills shown.** OpenCV (Gaussian blur, Canny, contours, bounding rectangles), NumPy, IoU.

## Can classic contour geometry confirm the CNN?

**How it was tested.** Each CNN prediction was checked against a contour-geometry classifier (vertex counts and shape measures).

| CNN and contour agree | Correct when they agree |
|---|---|
| 66.2% of images | 99.92% |

**Decision.** Agreement is shown on every output image: the located outline is drawn green when geometry agrees with the CNN and
red when it does not, so a reviewer can see which predictions a second, independent method backs.

**Source.** Output of `verify_accuracy.py` ("Geometry cross-check").

**Skills shown.** OpenCV contour analysis, PyTorch, image annotation.
